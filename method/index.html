Method

How the numbers are counted.

Kyiv air alerts

The count and cumulative time are working figures assembled from listed alert intervals. They are provisional, may differ between trackers, and exclude unresolved intervals where the start or end cannot be reconciled confidently.

Read the alert-count methodology

Dead civilians

The displayed figure is an incident-level subtotal drawn from official or media reporting on incidents recorded in the chronology. It is not a national total, and overlapping regional totals are not added where they could duplicate incident-level figures.

Read the civilian-death methodology

Facilities and bridge strikes

Facilities are counted as identified sites, not as a precise count of buildings. Repeated strikes on the same facility do not create new entries. Bridge-strike figures count reported hits, not a structural-damage assessment.

Facility-count methodology · Bridge-strike methodology

Data centers

The data-center chronology is maintained separately, with incident entries and source notes attached to the reports.
